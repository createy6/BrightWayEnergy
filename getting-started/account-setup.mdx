New to BrightWay Energy? This guide walks you through creating your account and getting your profile ready so you can start tracking energy usage and managing billing in minutes.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Sign up for BrightWay">
    Go to the [BrightWay Energy signup page](https://app.brightway.energy/signup) and enter your email address, create a password, and select your country. Click **Create Account** to continue.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Check your inbox for a verification email from BrightWay Energy. Click the **Verify Email** button in the message. If you do not see it, check your spam or promotions folder.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your property type">
    After verification, select whether your property is **Residential** or **Commercial**. This setting determines your dashboard layout, rate plans, and available integrations.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete your profile">
    Enter your property address, set your billing currency, and add a display name. Click **Finish Setup** to enter your dashboard.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Supported property types include single-family homes, apartments, small offices, retail spaces, and industrial facilities. If your property type is not listed, choose the closest match and update it later in **Account \> Profile**.
</Note>

Once setup is complete, you will land on your main dashboard where you can connect meters, set usage targets, and view real-time energy data.
