This guide walks you through your first five minutes with BrightWay Energy. By the end, you will have an active account, a connected smart meter, a usage target, enabled alerts, and your dashboard ready.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to the BrightWay Energy sign-up page and enter your email address and a secure password. Verify your email to activate the account. Choose between a residential or commercial plan based on your needs.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect your smart meter">
    In the dashboard, select **Integrations** and then **Smart Meters**. Choose your meter brand from the list and follow the pairing instructions. Once connected, real-time data will begin flowing into your account within a few minutes.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set an energy target">
    Navigate to **Energy** \> **Set Targets**. Enter a daily or monthly usage limit that matches your budget or sustainability goal. BrightWay will compare your live usage against this target automatically.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable alerts">
    Open **Energy** \> **Alerts** and turn on notifications for target breaches and unusual usage spikes. You can choose email, SMS, or push notifications depending on your plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your dashboard">
    Return to your main dashboard to see live consumption charts, target progress, and recent alerts. Your data updates in real time once the meter connection is active.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Some smart meter models may require a firmware update before pairing. If your device is not detected, check the manufacturer's app first.
</Note>
