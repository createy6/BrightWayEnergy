BrightWay Energy sends notifications across several categories so you stay informed without being overwhelmed. You control exactly which events reach you, and through which channels.

## Notification types

BrightWay offers four notification categories:

- **Billing**: Payment reminders, invoice availability, and payment failure alerts.
- **Alerts**: Usage target breaches, anomaly warnings, and threshold notifications.
- **Reports**: Weekly summaries, monthly usage reports, and efficiency insights.
- **System updates**: Scheduled maintenance, feature releases, and policy changes.

## Set your preferences

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your name in the top right corner, then select **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Notifications">
    Click the **Notifications** tab inside the Account section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose channels per category">
    For each notification type, toggle the channels you want: **Email**, **SMS**, or **Push** (if you have the mobile app installed).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save your settings">
    Click **Save Preferences** to apply your choices.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  You can disable a category entirely by turning off all three channels for that notification type.
</Tip>
