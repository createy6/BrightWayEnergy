Keep your BrightWay Energy account accurate and secure by keeping your profile information up to date. You can edit your name, contact details, and password directly in the dashboard.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your name in the top right corner of the dashboard, then select **Account** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Profile">
    Click the **Profile** tab inside the Account section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Update your details">
    Edit your name, email address, or phone number as needed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change your password (optional)">
    Click **Change Password**, enter your current password, then type and confirm your new password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save changes">
    Click **Save** at the bottom of the page to apply your updates.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  If you change your email address, BrightWay will send a verification message to the new address. You must confirm the new email before it becomes active on your account.
</Note>
