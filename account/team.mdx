Add colleagues to your BrightWay Energy account so they can monitor usage, review reports, or manage billing. Each member gets a role that controls what they can see and do.

## Invite a team member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Account Settings">
    Click your name in the top right corner, then select **Account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Go to Team">
    Click the **Team** tab inside the Account section.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send an invitation">
    Click **Invite Member**, enter the person's email address, and select a role from the dropdown.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Click **Send Invitation**. The invitee will receive an email with a link to join your account.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Roles and permissions

| Role | View usage & reports | Manage alerts & targets | Manage billing | Manage team & settings |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Owner | Yes | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Admin | Yes | Yes | Yes | No |
| Viewer | Yes | No | No | No |

<Warning>
  Only one team member can hold the Owner role. If you need to transfer ownership, the current Owner must reassign the role to another member from the Team page. This action is immediate and cannot be undone without the new Owner's consent.
</Warning>
