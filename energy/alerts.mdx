Alerts in BrightWay Energy keep you informed when your property's electricity consumption crosses a threshold you define. Use them to catch unexpected spikes early, avoid bill surprises, and reinforce the energy targets you have set.

## Create an alert rule

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Alerts settings">
    Log in to BrightWay Energy, click **Energy** in the top navigation, then select **Alerts** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new rule">
    Click **New Alert**. Give your rule a clear name, such as "High Daily Usage."
  </Step>
  <Step title="Define the condition">
    Choose the trigger type. For example, select **Usage** and enter a condition like **greater than** `50` **kWh/day**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose delivery channels">
    Pick how you want to be notified: **Email**, **SMS**, and/or **Push**. You can enable multiple channels for the same rule.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the rule">
    Click **Save**. The alert becomes active immediately and will fire the next time your usage meets the condition.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  SMS alerts may be subject to standard carrier messaging rates depending on your plan and location. We recommend enabling email or push notifications as a backup channel.
</Warning>

## Manage existing alerts

Return to **Energy \> Alerts** to view all active rules. Click any alert to edit its threshold or delivery settings, or click the trash icon to delete it. You can also temporarily disable a rule by toggling its **Active** switch without removing it.
