The Energy Management section in BrightWay Energy gives you full visibility into how your property consumes electricity. Use real-time monitoring, customizable targets, and proactive alerts to understand your patterns and lower your bills.

## What you can do

- View live and historical usage in kilowatt-hours (kWh)
- Set daily or monthly consumption targets
- Get notified when usage crosses your chosen thresholds
- Compare trends across days, weeks, and months
