The usage dashboard in BrightWay Energy shows exactly when and how your property consumes electricity. Use it to spot waste, verify the impact of new appliances, and plan changes that reduce your bill.

## Open the usage dashboard

<Steps>
  <Step title="Navigate to Energy">
    Log in to BrightWay Energy and click **Energy** in the top navigation bar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open Monitor Usage">
    Select **Monitor Usage** from the dropdown or sidebar menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your time range">
    Use the range picker at the top of the chart to switch between **Live**, **Today**, **This Week**, or **This Month**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Read the chart

The main chart shows consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh) over time.

- **Live view**: updates every 30 seconds with your current draw
- **Daily view**: breaks the day into hourly segments so you can see peak times
- **Weekly and monthly views**: show total consumption per day with an average line for quick comparison

Hover over any bar or point to see the exact kWh value for that period. A small summary panel below the chart displays your total for the selected range, your average per day, and your highest single hour.

<Tip>
  Compare today against yesterday or last week using the **Compare** toggle above the chart. This makes it easy to see if a recent change (like a new thermostat schedule) is saving energy.
</Tip>
