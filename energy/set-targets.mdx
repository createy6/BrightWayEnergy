Energy targets in BrightWay Energy help you stay within a planned budget by setting clear limits on how much electricity your property can use. Once a target is active, your dashboard shows progress bars and color-coded status so you know at a glance whether you are on track.

## Create a target

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Energy > Targets">
    Log in to BrightWay Energy, click **Energy** in the top navigation, then select **Targets** from the sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new target">
    Click **New Target**. Choose whether you want a **Daily** or **Monthly** limit.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your limit">
    Type your desired maximum consumption in kilowatt-hours (kWh). For example, enter `45` for a daily limit of 45 kWh.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and activate">
    Click **Save**. The target becomes active immediately and appears on your dashboard with a progress indicator.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Start with a target about 10% below your current average. You can find your average in **Monitor Usage** under the monthly view. A modest reduction is easier to maintain and still delivers meaningful savings.
</Tip>

## Edit or pause a target

To change a limit, return to **Energy \> Targets**, click the target card, update the kWh value, and click **Save**. To pause tracking without deleting the target, toggle the **Active** switch off. You can re-enable it at any time.
