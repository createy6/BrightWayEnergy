Your BrightWay Energy invoices are stored securely in the dashboard so you can review charges, track payments, and download copies anytime you need them. This guide walks you through finding an invoice, filtering your history, and saving a PDF.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Invoices page">
    Log in to your BrightWay dashboard. Click **Billing** in the main navigation, then select **Invoices** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Filter by date range">
    Use the date filter at the top of the invoice list to narrow results. Choose a preset range like **Last 3 months** or enter a custom start and end date. Press **Apply** to update the list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Preview an invoice">
    Click any invoice row to open the preview panel. The preview shows your billing period, total amount due, line-item charges, and payment status. Scroll down to see a full breakdown of usage and fees.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download the PDF">
    In the invoice preview panel, click the **Download PDF** button. The file saves to your default downloads folder with a name like `BrightWay_Invoice_2024-01.pdf`.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Invoices are generated on the 1st of each month and are available in your dashboard by 9:00 AM local time. If you do not see a recent invoice, check that your filter includes the current month or refresh the page after the generation time.
</Note>

## Invoice details explained

Each invoice preview contains these sections:

- **Billing period**: the start and end dates for the charges shown
- **Account summary**: your service address, meter numbers, and rate plan
- **Usage charges**: energy consumed multiplied by your rate, plus any demand charges
- **Fees and credits**: taxes, regulatory fees, and applied credits or promotions
- **Payment status**: whether the invoice is paid, pending, or overdue
