BrightWay Energy makes it easy to manage your entire billing experience from a single dashboard. Whether you are a residential customer tracking monthly charges or a commercial account managing multiple meters, the Billing section gives you full visibility into invoices, payment options, and detailed usage reports.

## What you can do

From the Billing area you can complete these core tasks:

- Review and download past invoices as PDFs
- Add, edit, and set default payment methods
- Export usage data in CSV or PDF format for any date range

## Invoice schedule

BrightWay generates invoices on the 1st of each month. Invoices are available in your dashboard by 9:00 AM local time on generation day. If you have questions about a specific charge, open the invoice preview and click **Contact Support** to start a billing inquiry.
