BrightWay Energy lets you export detailed usage data for any billing period or custom date range. These reports help you analyze consumption trends, verify charges, and share data with property managers or sustainability teams. You can download reports in CSV for spreadsheet analysis or PDF for presentations and records.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Reports page">
    Log in to your BrightWay dashboard. Click **Billing** in the main navigation, then select **Reports** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Select your date range">
    Use the date picker to choose the period you want to export. You can select a preset like **Last 30 days** or **Last billing period**, or enter a custom start and end date. Click **Apply** to confirm.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose your format">
    Select **CSV** if you plan to open the report in Excel, Google Sheets, or another analysis tool. Select **PDF** if you need a formatted document for sharing or printing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Export the report">
    Click the **Export** button. Your browser downloads the file immediately. Large date ranges may take a few seconds to generate.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Choose CSV when you want to sort, filter, or build charts from your usage data. PDF works best for sharing a clean summary with stakeholders who do not need raw numbers.
</Tip>

## What is included in a report

Each usage report contains the following data:

- **Timestamp**: the date and time of each reading
- **Energy consumed**: usage in kWh for the interval
- **Rate applied**: the per-kWh rate in effect at that time
- **Cost**: the calculated charge for that interval
- **Meter ID**: the identifier for the meter that recorded the data

Commercial accounts may also see demand readings and time-of-use rate breakdowns.

## Report limits

- Maximum date range per export: 12 months
- CSV exports include up to 100,000 rows per file
- If your range exceeds the row limit, split it into smaller date ranges and export separately
