You can manage all payment methods for your BrightWay Energy account from one page. Add new cards or bank accounts, update expired details, and choose a default method so your monthly invoices are paid automatically without manual intervention.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Payment Methods">
    Log in to your BrightWay dashboard. Click **Billing** in the main navigation, then select **Payment Methods** from the dropdown menu.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a new payment method">
    Click the **Add Payment Method** button. Choose **Card** or **Bank Account** depending on how you want to pay.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your details">
    For cards, enter the card number, expiration date, CVV, and billing ZIP code. For bank accounts, enter your routing number and account number. Review the information for accuracy, then click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set as default (optional)">
    After saving, toggle the **Default** switch next to the new payment method if you want BrightWay to use it for all future automatic charges. You can change the default at any time.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  BrightWay Energy stores payment data using PCI DSS Level 1 compliant infrastructure. Never email card numbers or bank details to support. If you need to update a payment method, always use the Payment Methods page in your dashboard.
</Warning>

## Manage existing methods

From the Payment Methods page you can also:

- **Edit** a saved card or bank account to update an expiration date or billing address
- **Remove** a method you no longer use, as long as it is not the only active payment method on the account
- **Reorder** methods by dragging them in the list (the top method becomes the default)

## Failed payments

If an automatic charge fails, BrightWay retries after 3 business days. You receive an email notification and a dashboard alert. To avoid service interruptions, add a backup payment method or update the failing one before the retry date.
