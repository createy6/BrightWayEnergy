If something is not working as expected in BrightWay Energy, use the steps below to identify the symptom and resolve the issue quickly. Each section covers a common problem with clear actions you can take.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="My smart meter is not syncing data">
    **Symptoms:** No recent usage data appears on your dashboard, or the last reading is more than a few hours old.

    **Resolution:**

    1. Confirm that your smart meter is powered on and connected to your Wi-Fi network.
    2. In the BrightWay dashboard, go to **Integrations \> Smart Meters**.
    3. Find your device and click **Reconnect**.
    4. If syncing does not resume, restart the meter using the manufacturer's instructions, then click **Reconnect** again.
    5. Wait up to 30 minutes for the next data interval to populate.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I cannot find my invoice">
    **Symptoms:** You expected an invoice but do not see it in your dashboard or email.

    **Resolution:**

    1. Check the **Billing \> Invoices** page in your dashboard and use the date filter to locate the billing period.
    2. Look in your email spam or promotions folder for a message from BrightWay Energy.
    3. If your billing cycle started recently, invoices may take up to 24 hours after cycle start to generate.
    4. Verify that your payment method is active under **Billing \> Payment Methods**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="My alert did not fire when usage spiked">
    **Symptoms:** Your usage exceeded a target or threshold, but you did not receive an alert.

    **Resolution:**

    1. Go to **Energy \> Alerts** and confirm the alert is toggled on.
    2. Check the alert conditions (threshold value, comparison operator, and time window) to make sure they match the expected trigger.
    3. Review your notification preferences under **Account \> Notifications** to ensure the alert channel (email, SMS, or push) is enabled.
    4. Note that alerts evaluate on a schedule; there may be a short delay between a usage spike and the notification.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="I am having trouble logging in">
    **Symptoms:** You cannot access your dashboard, or you see an authentication error.

    **Resolution:**

    1. Confirm you are using the correct email address associated with your account.
    2. Click **Forgot Password** on the login screen and follow the reset link sent to your email.
    3. If you use a password manager, verify that it is not auto-filling an old password.
    4. Clear your browser cache and cookies, then try logging in again.
    5. If you still cannot log in, check whether your account email needs re-verification after a recent change in **Account \> Profile**.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Still need help?

If the steps above do not resolve your issue, contact BrightWay support with the following details:

- Your account email address
- A brief description of the symptom
- Any error messages you see
- The steps you have already tried
