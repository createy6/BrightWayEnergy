Get quick answers to the most common questions about BrightWay Energy. If you need step-by-step help, follow the links to our detailed guides.

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="How do I create a BrightWay Energy account?">
    Visit the BrightWay Energy sign-up page and enter your email address, create a password, and confirm your email. After verification, you can connect your first meter and start tracking energy usage. For a full walkthrough, see the [Quickstart](/quickstart) guide.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Why is my smart meter not connecting?">
    Make sure your meter is powered on and within range of your Wi-Fi network. In the BrightWay dashboard, go to **Integrations \> Smart Meters** and click **Reconnect**. If the issue persists, reset your meter using the manufacturer's instructions and try again.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="When will I receive my invoice?">
    Invoices are generated on the first day of each billing cycle and sent to your registered email address. You can also view and download invoices at any time from the [Billing](/billing/view-invoices) section of your dashboard.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How do I cancel my BrightWay Energy subscription?">
    Go to **Account \> Billing**, scroll to the bottom of the page, and click **Cancel Subscription**. Follow the prompts to confirm. Your access will remain active until the end of the current billing period.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="How does BrightWay protect my energy data?">
    BrightWay encrypts all data in transit and at rest. We do not sell your personal or usage data to third parties. You can request a full data export or deletion at any time from **Account \> Privacy**.
  </Accordion>

  <Accordion title="Which smart devices are supported?">
    BrightWay supports most major smart meter brands, solar inverters with monitoring APIs, and select home energy monitors. Check the [Integrations Overview](/integrations/overview) for the full list of supported devices and setup instructions.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
