Connect your smart meter to BrightWay to import real-time and historical energy usage data directly into your dashboard. This lets you monitor consumption, set targets, and receive alerts without manual readings.

## Supported Meter Types

<Note>
  BrightWay supports **SMETS1** and **SMETS2** smart meters. If you are unsure which type you have, check the label on your meter or your energy supplier's documentation.
</Note>

## Connect Your Smart Meter

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In your BrightWay dashboard, open **Integrations** from the left sidebar, then select **Smart Meters**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Meter">
    Click **Add Meter** to start the connection process.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Meter Details">
    Type your meter serial number and select your energy supplier from the dropdown list.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorize Data Access">
    Review the permissions and click **Authorize** to allow BrightWay to read your meter data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm Connection">
    Wait for the confirmation message. Your meter data will appear in the dashboard within a few minutes.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What Happens Next

After a successful connection:

- Usage data syncs automatically throughout the day
- Historical data is imported for the past 12 months where available
- Your meter appears in the **Energy** and **Billing** sections

## Troubleshooting

- **Connection failed:** Double-check the serial number and supplier selection. Try again after a few minutes.
- **No data appearing:** Ensure your meter is active and transmitting. Contact your supplier if the meter itself shows no signal.
- **Wrong supplier listed:** Choose **Other** and enter your supplier name manually.
