Integrate your solar panel system with BrightWay to track generation, net metering, and self-consumption in real time. You can connect by entering your inverter details manually or by linking through a supported monitoring provider.

## Connect Your Solar System

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Integrations">
    In your BrightWay dashboard, open **Integrations** from the left sidebar, then select **Solar**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add a Solar System">
    Click **Add System** to begin the setup.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter Inverter or Provider Details">
    Choose your connection method:

    - **Direct inverter:** Enter the inverter serial number, model, and location.
    - **Supported provider:** Select your platform (for example, SolarEdge or Enphase) and sign in to authorize data access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save and Confirm">
    Click **Save**. BrightWay will verify the connection and begin importing your solar data.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What You Can Track

Once connected, your solar data appears across BrightWay:

- **Generation:** Real-time and historical production in kWh
- **Net metering:** Import and export to the grid
- **Self-consumption:** How much solar energy you use directly
- **Savings:** Estimated cost reductions based on your tariff

<Tip>
  If your utility supports net metering, BrightWay will display net usage alongside your solar generation so you can see exactly when you are drawing from or feeding into the grid.
</Tip>

## Troubleshooting

- **Provider login fails:** Verify your credentials on the provider's website first, then retry the connection in BrightWay.
- **Data is missing:** Some providers batch data and may delay updates by up to an hour.
- **Inverter not listed:** Choose **Other** and enter the manufacturer and model manually. Contact support if data still does not appear after 24 hours.
