## Supported Integrations

- **Smart Meters:** Import real-time and historical consumption and export data.
- **Solar Panels:** Track generation, net metering, and self-consumption from supported inverters and platforms.
- **More coming soon:** Battery storage, EV chargers, and additional IoT devices.

## What to Expect After Connecting

Connected devices typically sync within a few minutes. Once active, your data appears in:

- **Energy charts** for real-time and historical views
- **Billing summaries** for accurate cost calculations
- **Alerts** for anomalies or target deviations

<Tip>
  If your device is not listed, contact support to request compatibility updates. Response times are typically within one business day.
</Tip>
