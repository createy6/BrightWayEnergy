## How BrightWay Energy works

Getting up and running takes only a few steps. Follow this path to start managing your energy today.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect your meter">
    Link your smart meter or supported device so BrightWay can begin collecting real-time usage data.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set usage targets">
    Define daily, weekly, or monthly targets to keep consumption within budget.
  </Step>
  <Step title="View your dashboard">
    Explore live charts and historical trends on your personal dashboard.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Get alerts">
    Enable notifications so you know immediately when usage exceeds your targets.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Commercial customers can invite team members and assign roles from the Team Management page after completing setup.
</Tip>
